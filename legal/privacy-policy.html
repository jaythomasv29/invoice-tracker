Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Who we are

Sift helps restaurant operators organize supplier invoices, understand purchasing costs, and estimate recipe costs. For privacy requests, contact chimpchamp92@gmail.com. This policy covers the mobile app and its supporting services.

Information we process

We process your email address and account identifiers; restaurant name and organization identifiers; invoice photos and extracted supplier names, addresses, account numbers, line items, prices, quantities and dates; dish names, optional dish photos and recipe ingredients; subscription status and purchase identifiers; and technical crash diagnostics. Only upload documents and images you are authorized to share.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, separate restaurant records, extract and store invoice details, compare prices, calculate spending and recipe estimates, verify subscriptions, enforce usage limits, respond to support requests and improve reliability. We do not sell your information. Sift contains no advertising SDKs.

AI processing and your permission

With your permission, invoice images and their contents, or dish names and optional dish images, are sent to Anthropic (Claude) for invoice extraction or recipe drafting. Photos may contain personal and commercial information. Sift asks before this processing. You can decline or withdraw permission in Profile; new AI requests will stop and saved records remain available. AI results can be incorrect and should be reviewed. Provider processing and retention depend on the service terms applicable to our account; contact us for details.

Service providers

Clerk provides account authentication and organization membership. Supabase stores restaurant records and photos and runs backend functions. Anthropic processes AI requests. Apple processes App Store payments; RevenueCat verifies purchase and subscription entitlements using restaurant and transaction identifiers. Sentry receives technical diagnostics to help fix errors; Sift filters diagnostic event content to avoid sending invoice images and extracted details. The website is hosted on Vercel. Providers process data as needed to supply these services.

Storage and retention

Active restaurant records are retained to provide history and comparisons. Deleting an invoice queues its stored images for removal. Account deletion removes your authentication account and applicable personal records. Restaurants where you are the only member are deleted with their invoice and recipe data and photos; shared business records remain with remaining members. Pending deletions are retried by our backend. Minimal deletion-status records are retained for up to 30 days for retry and audit purposes. Provider backups may expire on a different schedule and are not used to restore deleted records into normal service.

Your choices

Use Profile to export invoice history as CSV, change AI processing permission, manage an Apple subscription, or delete your account. If you are the only administrator of a shared restaurant, arrange another administrator before deleting your account. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription: cancel through Apple subscription settings. For access, correction, a shared-organization deletion request, or a problem completing deletion, contact chimpchamp92@gmail.com. We may need to verify your authority over the restaurant.

Security and international processing

We use authenticated access, organization-scoped database policies and private image storage. Service providers may process data outside your country. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

Children and changes

Sift is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16. We will update this policy when processing changes and request new AI permission when materially needed. Contact chimpchamp92@gmail.com with questions.
